1.	Pobo bokware, kodi icare Boe ere jeturudo kurugugoe etae, ewo todo to torito, pobo, bogai.

2.	Icare ere todo to torito, pobo bogai mare ekare pobo arego, kodi icare Boe ere jeturudo Cugui ae, uwo to torito, pobo bogai.

3.	Icare ure todo to torito, ema koiare ure pobo arego tori piji, dutabore icare pobore tuiodo boe bukeje.

4.	Ure okodu i butudo, ure bokwado i butudo, ure burudu i butudo, ure noido butudo, ure apido butudo, ure boe jamedu butudo rugadu.

5.	Icare pobo  barire, pobo  kurire, mare jetorogodunure.

6.	Jure koiare ure pobo  mekido pugeje.

7.	Ure tuia mugudo, pobo  mekire, ure tuiedorodo, pobo jetorogodure.

8.	Kodire Boe egore Jure koiaie pobo  uia mugudo oino tuia mugu ureore, ure pobo  uia mugudo.

9.  Duwuge pagire padure kugaru jipa rairewu tabo, toriguru ji jipa rairewu tabo, kiworo jipa rairewu tabo, iaga i guru jipa rairewu tabo, togoriwo jipa rairewu tabo.

10.	Baitogogo mare Mano Kurirewu emagere ero ino.

11.	Iage rema eture pugeje tumanamage eregodaji, kugaru jipa koborirewu, toriguru jipa koborirewu, kiworo jipa koborirewu, iaga i guru jipa koborirewu, tugoriwo jipa koborirewu tabo.

12.	Tadugo mare Butore Agadu emagere ero ino tumanamage seus eregodaji.

13.	Maragaduie Kurugugoe ere adugo burea to nowu kugaru ji, ere aigo burea to, ere aipoburewu burea to jamedu.

14.	Dukodie Boe egore adugo burea paduie, tu kugaru keje, aigo burea paduie, tu kugaru keje, aipoburewu burea paduie, tu kugaru keje, oinore Boe egore.

15.	Boe egore Tuguri Ecebage etuie, tuguri ito guru tabo, pugeje, ekodo  ji...Jarudori paru kae.

16.	Nonore ere tuguri ito guru oto bu nono, rugadu.

17.	Awu pobo jamedu akedu keje, ere tugeragu Okwagijedogei ere tugeragu Kawagijedoge ji, ere tugeragu Ipie Kurudoge ji.

18.	Nowugere Baitogogore, Mano Kurirewure eeto poboji pudai reciprocidade.

19.	Ure pobo tawujewu, mugo tu ewugajeje eles, Cugui iere Waregia jamedu.
